# Credit notes and refunding clients

## Credit notes

A credit note reduces what a client owes on an invoice. Use it to correct a locked invoice or to give
a discount after the fact. A credit note can be applied to the original invoice or kept as credit for
the client's next invoice.

## Refunding a payment

You can refund a card payment from the invoice page, in full or in part, within 120 days of the
payment. The card processing fee on the original payment isn't returned.

Payments made by bank transfer (ACH) can't be refunded through Tallyfox. Send the money back from your
own bank and record the refund on the invoice with **Record refund**.

This article is about refunds you give your clients. To ask for a refund of your Tallyfox subscription,
see [Your Tallyfox subscription](account-billing.md).
