# Invoicing in other currencies

On the Growth and Scale plans you can invoice clients in more than 40 currencies. Set the currency on
each client; new invoices for that client use it.

## Getting paid in another currency

Your clients pay in the invoice currency. We convert the payment to your home currency at the
exchange rate on the day of the payment and pay it out as usual.

Card payments in a currency other than your home currency have a 1% conversion fee, on top of the
normal card fee. Bank transfer (ACH) payments are only available for invoices in US dollars.

## Reports

Reports show amounts in your home currency, using the rate on the day each payment was made.
