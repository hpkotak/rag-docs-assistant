# Taxes on invoices

## Tax rates

Create tax rates in **Settings > Taxes** (for example "VAT 20%" or "CA sales tax 7.25%"). Starter lets
you apply one tax rate per line item; Growth and Scale allow two, for example a state and a city tax.

## Tax-inclusive prices

Turn on **Prices include tax** if your line item prices already include tax. Tallyfox then shows the
tax portion separately without adding it on top.

## Tax numbers

Add your VAT or GST number in **Settings > Business profile** to show it on every invoice. You can
also store each client's tax number. For EU business clients in another EU country, turn on
**Reverse charge** on the client to add the reverse charge note and apply 0% VAT.

## Filing

Tallyfox doesn't file or pay taxes for you. Use the tax report in **Reports > Taxes** to see the tax
you've collected for any period, and give it to your accountant.
