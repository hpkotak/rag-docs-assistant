# Payment reminders and late fees

## Automatic reminders

Tallyfox can email clients about unpaid invoices so you don't have to chase them. Turn reminders on in
**Settings > Reminders**. You can send up to 4 reminders per invoice, at any of these times:

- 3 days before the due date
- on the due date
- 7 days after the due date
- 14 days after the due date

Reminders stop as soon as the invoice is paid in full.

## Late fees

You can add a late fee to overdue invoices, either a fixed amount or a percentage of the unpaid
balance.

- The fee is added once for each month the invoice stays overdue, up to 3 times per invoice.
- Percentage fees are calculated on the original unpaid balance, not including earlier late fees.
- You can set a grace period of 0 to 30 days after the due date before the first fee is added.

Check local rules before charging late fees; some places limit them.
