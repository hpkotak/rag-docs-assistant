# Contacting support

- **Email:** support@tallyfox.example, all plans. We reply within 1 business day (within 4 hours on Scale).
- **Live chat:** Growth and Scale, Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 US Eastern time. Open it from the
  help icon in the app.

Phone support isn't available on any plan.

For account security reasons, we can only discuss billing with the account Owner. We will never ask
for your password or full card number.
