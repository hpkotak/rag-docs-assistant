# Sign-in and security

## Ways to sign in

- **Email and password:** all plans.
- **Sign in with Google:** all plans.
- **SAML single sign-on (SSO):** Scale only. Works with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), OneLogin
  and other SAML 2.0 providers. Set it up in **Settings > Security > Single sign-on**.

## Two-factor authentication

Anyone can turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, on any plan. On Growth and
Scale, Admins can also require it for everyone on the team.

## How we protect your data

- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256).
- Tallyfox has a SOC 2 Type II report, available under NDA. Ask your account contact or support.
- Card details are handled by our PCI DSS Level 1 payment processor and never stored by Tallyfox.

## Data regions

Accounts are hosted in the United States by default. On Scale, you can choose to host your account in
the EU (Frankfurt) when you create it.
