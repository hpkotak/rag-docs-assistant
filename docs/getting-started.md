# Getting started with Tallyfox

Tallyfox lets small businesses send invoices, get paid online and keep track of who owes what.

## Set up your business profile

1. Go to **Settings > Business profile** and add your business name, address and tax number.
2. Upload your logo (PNG or SVG). It appears on invoices, emails and the client portal.
3. Choose your home currency. This is the currency your payouts are made in, and it can't be changed
   after your first payout.

## Connect payments

To accept card and bank transfer payments, go to **Settings > Payments** and verify your business.
Verification usually takes 1 to 2 business days. You can send invoices before verification is
complete; clients just can't pay online until it is.

## Send your first invoice

Go to **Invoices > New invoice**, pick or create a client, add line items and click **Send**. See
[Creating and editing invoices](invoices-create.md) for details.

## Invite your team

Go to **Settings > Team** to invite people. See [Team members and roles](team-roles.md).
