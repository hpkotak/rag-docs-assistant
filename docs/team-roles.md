# Team members and roles

Invite people in **Settings > Team**. How many team members your plan includes is listed in
[Plans and pricing](plans-and-pricing.md).

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing, plan changes and deleting the account. Each account has exactly one Owner. |
| Admin | Everything except billing, plan changes and deleting the account. Can manage team members and API keys. |
| Member | Create, send and edit invoices and clients, and record payments. Can't see reports or change settings. |
| Accountant | Read-only access to invoices, clients, payments and reports, and can export them. Can't create, send or edit anything. |

Only Owners and Admins can change someone's role.

## Transferring ownership

The Owner can make an existing Admin the new Owner in **Settings > Team**. The previous Owner becomes an
Admin. If the Owner has left and can't do this, contact support with proof that you're authorised to
act for the business.

## Removing a team member

Removing someone signs them out immediately. The invoices they created stay in the account.
