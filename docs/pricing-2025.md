## Plan limits

- **Starter:** 30 invoices per month, 1 team member, up to 50 clients.
- **Growth:** 300 invoices per month, 3 team members, unlimited clients, multi-currency invoicing.
- **Scale:** unlimited invoices, 10 team members, recurring invoices, API access, priority support.

Extra team members cost $10 per member per month on Growth and Scale.
