# Integrations

Connect integrations in **Settings > Integrations**. Integrations are available on all plans.

## QuickBooks Online

Two-way sync of invoices, clients and payments, every 15 minutes. If a client exists in both, they're
matched by email address.

## Xero

One-way sync from Tallyfox to Xero: invoices and payments you create in Tallyfox appear in Xero.
Changes made in Xero aren't copied back to Tallyfox, and Xero contacts aren't imported.

## Zapier

Use Tallyfox triggers in your Zaps: **Invoice paid**, **Invoice overdue** and **New client**.
Actions (for example creating an invoice from a Zap) aren't available yet.

## Slack

Post a message to a Slack channel when an invoice is paid or becomes overdue.
