# Recurring invoices

Recurring invoices are available on the Scale plan.

A recurring invoice sends a copy of the same invoice to a client on a schedule, so you don't have to
create it again each time.

## Schedules

Choose weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly or yearly. Each invoice is created on the scheduled
date at 9:00 in your account's time zone and sent immediately.

## Ending a schedule

A schedule can run until you stop it, until a set end date, or for a set number of invoices.

## Automatic charging

If the client has saved a card in the client portal, you can turn on **Charge automatically**. The
card is charged on the invoice date, and the client gets a receipt instead of an invoice. If the charge
fails, the invoice is sent normally and the client is asked to pay it.

Recurring invoices can't be set up or edited in the mobile app.
