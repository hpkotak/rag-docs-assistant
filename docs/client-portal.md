# The client portal

Every invoice email has a link to the client portal, where your client can:

- see and pay their invoices
- download invoice PDFs and attachments
- save a card for automatic payments

Clients don't need an account; the link is unique to them.

## Branding

On all plans, the portal shows your logo and your brand colour. On Growth and Scale, you can also
remove the "Powered by Tallyfox" footer from the portal and invoice emails.

## Custom domain

On Scale, the portal can use your own domain, such as `billing.yourcompany.com`. Add a CNAME record
pointing to `portal.tallyfox.example` and enter the domain in **Settings > Client portal**.
