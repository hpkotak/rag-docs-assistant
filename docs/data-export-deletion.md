# Exporting and deleting your data

## Exporting

In **Settings > Export** you can download CSV files of your invoices, clients, payments and credit
notes at any time, on any plan. You can also download invoice PDFs as a ZIP file, up to 1,000
invoices per download.

## Deleting your account

Only the Owner can delete the account, in **Settings > Billing > Delete account**. Your data is kept
for 30 days after you delete the account, in case you change your mind, and is then permanently erased.
Backups are cleared within a further 90 days.

In many countries you're required to keep copies of invoices for several years. Export them before you
delete your account.
