# Payment methods and fees

Your clients can pay invoices online with:

- **Cards:** Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Apple Pay and Google Pay are offered automatically
  on supported devices.
- **Bank transfer (ACH):** for clients with a US bank account.
- **PayPal:** connect your PayPal business account in **Settings > Payments**.

## Fees

| Method | Fee per payment |
| --- | --- |
| Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| American Express | 3.5% + $0.30 |
| Bank transfer (ACH) | 0.8%, capped at $5 |
| PayPal | PayPal's own fees; Tallyfox adds nothing |

Fees are taken from each payment before it's paid out. There are no monthly fees for accepting payments.

## Passing fees on to clients

Where it's legal, you can add a card surcharge to cover the fee. Surcharges can't be added to bank
transfer payments.
