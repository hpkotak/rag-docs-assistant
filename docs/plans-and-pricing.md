# Plans and pricing

Tallyfox has three plans. Prices are in US dollars, per month, and don't include sales tax.

| Plan | Monthly price |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | $15 |
| Growth | $39 |
| Scale | $99 |

## Annual billing

Pay once a year and get 2 months free: you pay for 10 months and get 12. You can switch from monthly
to annual billing at any time in **Settings > Billing**.

## What each plan includes

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invoices per month | 50 | 500 | Unlimited |
| Team members included | 1 | 5 | 20 |
| Extra team members | Not available | $8 per member per month | $6 per member per month |
| Clients | 100 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Recurring invoices | No | Yes | Yes |
| Multi-currency invoicing | No | Yes | Yes |
| API access | No | Yes | Yes |
| Custom sending domain | No | Yes | Yes |
| SAML single sign-on | No | No | Yes |
| Choice of data region (US or EU) | No | No | Yes |
| Custom domain for the client portal | No | No | Yes |
| Support | Email | Email and live chat | Priority, first reply within 4 hours |

## Free trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No card is needed to start.
When the trial ends you choose a plan; if you don't, the account becomes read-only until you do.
