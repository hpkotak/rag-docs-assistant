# Changelog

Product changes, newest first. Where a help article hasn't been updated yet, the changelog entry is
the current behaviour.

## 2026-07-01: New plans and pricing

Starter is now $15, Growth $39 and Scale $99 per month, with more included on every plan. Annual
billing now gives 2 months free instead of 15% off. Existing customers keep their current price until
their next renewal after 2026-10-01. See [Plans and pricing](plans-and-pricing.md).

## 2026-06-15: Recurring invoices on Growth

Recurring invoices, previously available only on Scale, are now included in the Growth plan.

## 2026-05-20: Higher API rate limit on Scale

The API rate limit on Scale is now 300 requests per minute, up from 120. Growth stays at 60 requests
per minute.

## 2026-04-02: Faster bank transfer payouts

Payouts for payments made by bank transfer (ACH) now arrive in 2 business days instead of 3.

## 2026-03-10: PayPal retired for new accounts

New accounts can no longer connect PayPal. Accounts that already have PayPal connected can keep
accepting PayPal payments until December 31, 2026.

## 2026-02-01: Slack notifications

Send a message to a Slack channel when an invoice is paid or becomes overdue. See
[Integrations](integrations.md).
