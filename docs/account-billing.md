# Your Tallyfox subscription

This article is about paying for Tallyfox itself. Manage it in **Settings > Billing** (Owner only).

## Changing plans

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You pay the difference for the rest of your billing period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of your current billing period. If you're using more than the
  new plan allows (for example more team members than it includes), remove the extras before the
  downgrade takes effect, or it won't go through.

## Paying for Tallyfox

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Scale customers on annual billing can also pay by bank
transfer; contact support to set this up.

## Refunds

- **Annual plans:** full refund if you ask within 14 days of paying. After 14 days, annual plans
  aren't refundable, but you keep access until the end of the year you paid for.
- **Monthly plans:** not refundable. Cancel any time and you keep access until the end of the month.

To ask for a refund, use live chat or email support@tallyfox.example from the Owner's email address.

## Cancelling

Cancel in **Settings > Billing**. After the paid period ends the account becomes read-only for 90
days so you can export your data, and is then deleted.
