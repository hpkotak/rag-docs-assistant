# Payouts

Money your clients pay online is sent to your bank account automatically every business day.

## How long payouts take

| Payment method | Arrives in your bank |
| --- | --- |
| Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay | 2 business days |
| Bank transfer (ACH) | 3 business days |

Your first payout is held for 7 days while we finish verifying your business.

## Where payouts go

Payouts go to one bank account in your home currency. We support bank accounts in the US, the UK and
the EU. Change your payout account in **Settings > Payments**; changes take effect from the next
payout.

## Minimum payout

Payouts are made when your balance is at least $1.
