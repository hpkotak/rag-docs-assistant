# Creating and editing invoices

## Invoice numbers

Invoices are numbered automatically, starting at INV-0001. You can change the prefix and the next
number in **Settings > Invoices**, but you can't reuse a number that has already been sent.

## Payment terms

Choose when an invoice is due: on receipt, Net 7, Net 15, Net 30, Net 60, or a custom date. The
default for new clients is Net 30, and you can change the default per client.

## Attachments

You can attach PDF, PNG and JPG files to an invoice, such as a signed quote or a timesheet. Clients
can download attachments from the client portal.

## Drafts

Invoices are saved as drafts until you send them. Drafts don't count towards your monthly invoice limit.

## Editing an invoice after sending it

You can edit a sent invoice until a payment is recorded against it; the client sees the updated
version the next time they open it. Once any payment has been recorded, including a partial payment,
the invoice is locked. To correct it, issue a credit note (see
[Credit notes and refunds](credit-notes-refunds.md)) and, if needed, a new invoice.

## Deleting invoices

Drafts can be deleted. Sent invoices can't be deleted, only voided, so your numbering has no gaps.
