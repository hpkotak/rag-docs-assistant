# The Tallyfox mobile app

The Tallyfox app is available for iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android phones (Android 12 or later).

## What you can do in the app

- Create, send and edit invoices
- Record payments
- See who owes you money
- Get notifications when an invoice is paid

Drafts you create without a connection are saved and synced the next time you're online.

## Not available in the app

These need the web app: setting up recurring invoices, integrations, team management, API keys and
billing.
